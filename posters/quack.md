Duckweed is the fastest-growing flowering plant and is rich in protein, but
growing it cleanly at scale takes constant attention. QUACK is an open-source
kit that grows duckweed in a closed, sterilizable loop, monitors it with
sensors, and harvests it robotically, so research cultures stay clean and
growth is repeatable.
