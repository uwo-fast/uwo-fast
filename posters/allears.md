Children's ears grow quickly, so a child's hearing-aid earmold can stop
fitting within months. ALLEars uses AI to predict how a child's ear will grow,
so the next earmold can be ready before it is needed. The predicted models come
to the FAST lab, where we print them and develop low-cost manufacturing methods
to share openly with hearing-care providers worldwide.
