Freeze-drying (lyophilization) makes vaccines, medicines and diagnostics
shelf-stable without a cold chain, but lab freeze dryers are costly and hard to
service. SUBLIME is an open-source lyophilizer designed to be built, run and
repaired locally, with every drawing, part list and line of firmware published.
