Mushrooms turn agricultural and food waste into protein, in the dark, in a
small space. BLOOM is an open-source grow chamber built from standard baking
sheets, with controlled climate and filtered air, for resilient and
decentralized food production. It continues the lab's work on foods that keep
people fed when conventional farming fails.
