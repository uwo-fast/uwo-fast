Airborne infections spread fastest in shared indoor spaces. HaloAir is an
open-source device that keeps a low, measured level of glycol vapour in a
room's air, using its own sensors to hold the dose in range. The aim is a
low-cost, verifiable tool for making shared rooms safer, one room at a time.
