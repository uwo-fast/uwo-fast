Plant experiments are only as good as the chamber they run in, and commercial
growth chambers are expensive and closed. SPROUT is an open-source chamber
that controls light, climate, CO₂ and nutrients and logs all of it, so an
experiment run in one lab can be repeated in another. It builds on the lab's
agrivoltaics and controlled-environment growing work.
