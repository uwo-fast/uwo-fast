FAST, the Free Appropriate Sustainability Technology group, designs low-cost,
open-source hardware that anyone can build, repair and adapt. We work on solar
power and agrivoltaics, distributed recycling and 3-D printing, scientific
equipment, frugal medical devices and resilient food, and we publish
everything openly so it can be used where it is needed most.
