# Soldering Station

## Before You Start

- Use the soldering station only if you know how to solder safely or are being supervised.
- The room is ventilated; if your work needs local fume extraction, ask the PI or a post-doc first.
- Eye protection is recommended, especially when clipping leads, desoldering, or handling springy or tensioned parts.
- Keep drinks, food, loose paper, and unnecessary items away from the station.
- Check that the iron, stand, cord, tip, sponge/brass wool, and work surface are in safe condition.

## While Soldering

- Treat the iron, tip, solder, and nearby work as hot.
- Keep the iron in its stand whenever it is not in your hand.
- Keep wires, sleeves, plastic, and flammable material away from the hot tip.
- Use the correct solder, flux, tip, and temperature; check the solder composition first, as leaded solder may be present.
- Stop if the station smells wrong, smokes excessively, sparks, has damaged wiring, or behaves unexpectedly.

## When Done

- Turn the station off.
- Return the iron to its stand.
- Clean the tip on the sponge or brass wool, tin it with a little solder, then let the iron cool safely.
- Put tools, solder, flux, and small parts back where they belong.
- Sort waste: e-waste to its bin, non-hazardous to garbage, and hazardous solder/flux/contaminated waste to the hazardous-waste container or ask.
- Wash your hands after soldering, especially if leaded solder may have been used.

## Ask First

- Ask before changing station settings you do not understand.
- Ask before soldering batteries, high-power wiring, unknown components, or chemically contaminated items.
- Report damaged tips, missing tools, poor ventilation, or unsafe setup.
