Scope: Shared-use reminders for hand tools such as flush cutters, pliers, screwdrivers, wrenches, knives, and small cutters.

## Use The Right Tool

- Match the tool to the material, size, and force required.
- If a tool is flexing, slipping, denting, cracking, or taking excessive force, stop and choose a different tool.
- Do not use precision flush cutters on hard wire, steel rod, screws, nails, thick plastic, or anything beyond their rating.
- Do not use screwdrivers as pry bars, pliers as hammers, cutters as wire strippers, or knives as general-purpose scrapers.
- If you are not sure which tool is right, ask the PI or a post-doc.

## Before And During Use

- Inspect blades, jaws, handles, insulation, and moving parts before use.
- Cut away from yourself and others.
- Keep fingers out of pinch, cut, and snap-back zones.
- Use eye protection when cutting wire, clipping leads, trimming brittle material, or working with springy parts.
- Clamp or support the work when practical.

## When Done

- Put tools back on the counter holders, cabinets, or south-wall hex pegboard holders in TEB7.
- Clean debris, wire ends, and offcuts from the bench.
- Report damaged, dull, cracked, loose, or unsafe tools.

## Damaged Tools

- Stop using damaged tools immediately.
- Leave a clear note on the tool if it could injure the next user.
- Tell the PI or a post-doc so the tool can be repaired, replaced, or removed.
