# Power Tools

Scope: Shared-use reminders for portable power tools in the FAST lab. Use only if trained and authorized.

## Before You Start

- Choose the right tool, bit, blade, disc, abrasive, speed, and setup for the material.
- Inspect the tool, guard, cord, battery, switch, chuck, bit, blade, or disc before use.
- Wear required eye, hearing, dust, face, and hand protection for the task.
- Tie back long hair and keep loose clothing, jewelry, lanyards, and gloves away from rotating tools unless a task-specific procedure requires otherwise.
- Clamp or secure the workpiece before cutting, drilling, grinding, or sanding.
- Check what is behind or under the work before cutting or drilling.

## Tool Selection

| Tool | Use for | Ask first / avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drill driver | Drilling holes and light screw driving | High-torque fastening, unknown materials, or work that cannot be clamped. |
| Impact driver | Driving screws, bolts, and fasteners that need high torque | Precision drilling, fragile parts, or fasteners/materials that may strip or crack. |
| Angle grinder | Cutting or grinding compatible materials with the correct guard and disc | Changing discs, cutting unknown materials, removing guards, or using damaged discs. |
| Circular saw | Straight cuts in compatible sheet or lumber-like materials | Unsupported stock, warped material, wrong blade, or work that can pinch and kick back. |
| Sander | Surface finishing with the correct abrasive and dust control | Unknown coatings, hazardous dust, wet material, or material that may melt or ignite. |

## While Using A Tool

- Keep both hands, cords, clamps, and bystanders clear of the cutting or rotating path.
- Let the tool reach speed before contacting the work.
- Do not force the tool. Stop if it binds, grabs, overheats, vibrates, smokes, smells wrong, or sounds wrong.
- Keep guards in place and working.
- Wait for the tool to stop completely before setting it down.
- Keep dust, sparks, chips, and offcuts contained and away from people, chemicals, resin, and flammables.

## When Done

- Turn off and unplug the tool or remove the battery before changing bits, blades, discs, or abrasives.
- Put tools, batteries, chargers, bits, blades, discs, clamps, and PPE back where they belong.
- Clean dust, chips, scraps, and offcuts from the area.
- Remove damaged tools from service if you can do so safely, label them if local practice allows, and report damaged tools, missing guards, dull blades, cracked discs, frayed cords, battery damage, or unsafe behavior.

## Stop And Ask

- Ask before using angle grinders, circular saws, or any tool you have not been trained on.
- Ask before cutting, grinding, sanding, or drilling unknown material.
- Ask before making sparks, dust, fumes, or noise that could affect nearby people or work.
- Ask before working alone with a tool that can cause serious injury.
