# Chemical and Materials Storage

- chemical storage: acids right lower cabinet of fumehood and lab flammables and bases in left, theres a separate flammable cabinet thats yellow behind and to the left of the hood (this one should hold the spray paints, lubricants, other hardware store type flammables while the main flammable cabinet below the hood holds lab grade solvents; "dirty" / hardware store flammables opposed to lab grade flammables)
  - FOR THE LOVE OF EVERYTHING DO NOT PUT BASES IN THE ACID CABINET! This is the equivalent of making a thermo-bomb in the lab and is a serious safety hazard and will result in remands and mandatory retraining on chemical storage and handling procedures in addition to all the other consequences as left to the discretion of the lab manager and Dr Pearce. If you are unsure about where to store a chemical, ALWAYS ASK the lab manager or Dr Pearce before putting it in a cabinet.
