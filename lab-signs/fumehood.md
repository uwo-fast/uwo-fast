# Fume Hood

Status: draft
DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR POSTING
Review owner: FAST lab manager
Last updated: 2026-07-01

Scope: Use reminders for the FAST lab chemical fume hood. Use only if trained and authorized.

## Before Use

- Use the fume hood for work with volatile chemicals, hazardous vapors, or fumes when the approved procedure requires it.
- Ask before using the hood for powders, reactions, heating, pressure/vacuum setups, or unfamiliar hazards.
- Check that the hood is operating normally before starting: no alarm, not posted out of service, visible certification/current status acceptable, sash moves freely, and airflow is not blocked.
- Do not use the hood if it is alarming, damaged, blocked, expired/out of service, or behaving unexpectedly.
- Keep only the materials needed for the current work in the hood.

## While Working

- Keep containers closed except when actively adding or removing material.
- Work inside the hood, not at the front edge.
- Keep the sash as low as practical while still working safely.
- Do not put your head inside the hood.
- Do not use the hood as storage.
- Do not use the hood as a chemical disposal method, biological safety cabinet, or explosion-proof enclosure.

## When Done

- Close and label all containers.
- Clean the work surface and remove your materials.
- Return waste to the correct approved container or process.
- Lower the sash when you are finished.

## Utility Lines

Confirm the line before connecting anything.

| Color | Utility |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow | Vacuum |
| Orange | Air |
| Green | Cold water |

## Ask First

- Ask before connecting vacuum, air, cooling water, or unusual equipment setups.
- Ask before heating, evaporating, reacting, or storing anything in the hood.
- If you are unsure whether the hood is required or suitable, stop and ask the lab manager, responsible owner, post-doc, or Dr. Pearce.
