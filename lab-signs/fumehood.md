# Fume Hood

## Before Use

- Use the fume hood for work with volatile chemicals, hazardous vapors, or fumes when the approved procedure requires it.
- Ask before using the hood for reactions, heating, pressure/vacuum setups, or unfamiliar hazards.
- Check the digital display on the right side of the hood before starting: normal airflow, no warnings, no alarms, sash moves freely, and airflow is not blocked.
- Do not use the hood if it is alarming, damaged, blocked, expired/out of service, or behaving unexpectedly.
- Keep only the materials needed for the current work in the hood.
- Fume hoods run at all times. Do not switch one off.

## While Working

- Keep containers closed except when actively adding or removing material.
- Work inside the hood, not at the front edge.
- Keep the sash closed except when adjusting equipment or working inside. Raise it 30 to 45 cm (12 to 18 in) for that work, no higher.
- Keep apparatus at least 15 cm (6 in) inside the sash opening, and raise bulky items slightly so air can pass underneath.
- Do not put your head inside the hood.
- Do not use the hood as storage.
- Do not use the hood as a chemical disposal method, biological safety cabinet, or explosion-proof enclosure.
- NEVER use perchloric acid in this hood. Its FRP liner is not rated for it.
- Do not block the bottom airfoil or the baffle openings at the back of the hood. Keep the slots clear of dust, and keep paper, foil, and paper towel from being drawn into the duct.
- Do not adjust the baffles. That is work for qualified airflow or safety personnel.
- Powders need extra care: hood turbulence can throw them back out. Work well inside the hood and keep the sash low.
- Temporary waste containers are allowed only during active work, and only if they do not block the working area, airflow, sash movement, or safe use of the hood.

## When Done

- Close and label all containers.
- Clean the work surface and remove your materials.
- Return waste to the correct approved container or process.
- Lower the sash when you are finished.

## Utility Lines

Confirm the line before connecting anything.

| Color | Utility |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow | Vacuum |
| Orange | Air |
| Green | Cold water |

## Alarm Or Warning

- If the hood alarms, lower the sash first; the alarm is often caused by the sash being too high for the hood to maintain rated airflow.
- If lowering the sash does not clear the alarm, close open containers, make reactions safe, close the sash, leave the area, and contact the PI or a post-doc. An exhaust-system failure may exist.
- Airflow can be checked by Facilities Management Service Centre at extension 83304.
- If the hood is out of service, do not use it until it has been inspected and cleared for use.

## Ask First

- Ask before connecting vacuum, air, cooling water, or unusual equipment setups. Nothing may be connected to or modified on the hood without Facilities Management approval.
- Ask before weighing or handling a high-hazard powder. That needs a vented balance enclosure, not this hood.
- Ask before heating, evaporating, reacting, or storing anything in the hood.
- If you are unsure whether the hood is required or suitable, stop and ask the PI, a post-doc, or the responsible equipment owner.
