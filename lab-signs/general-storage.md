Scope: Shared storage reminders for non-chemical materials, parts, tools, and general supplies in the FAST lab.

## General Rules

- Do not store materials in sinks, fume hoods, walkways, electrical panels, emergency equipment areas, or active work surfaces.
- Label stored items if ownership, date, material, or purpose is not obvious.
- Keep reusable stock clean, findable, and realistically useful.
- Do not keep mystery scraps, unlabeled containers, contaminated parts, or abandoned project debris.
- If something is chemical, contaminated, sharp, hazardous, or unknown, follow the appropriate sign or ask before storing it.

## Red Standing Cabinets

- FAST is moving away from chemical storage in TEB7.
- Use red standing cabinets and general shelves for non-chemical materials, shared items, and clean storage only.
- Glassware, strainers, funnels, and similar shared items may be stored in the red standing cabinet bottom drawer if that location remains current.
- Do not put chemicals in these cabinets unless the PI or a post-doc explicitly approves the location.

## Reuse Or Discard

| Item | Keep? | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean simple plates, blocks, containers, or stock | Maybe | Keep only if likely to be reused. |
| Complex offcuts with unique holes, embedded parts, adhesives, or contamination | Usually no | Reuse immediately or dispose correctly. |
| Unknown material | No | Identify it or ask. |
| Abandoned project material | Maybe | Ask owner or PI/post-doc before discarding. |
